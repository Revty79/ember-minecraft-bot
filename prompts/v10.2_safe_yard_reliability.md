# v10.2 Safe Yard Reliability

## Goal
Repair and harden safe yard wandering so EMBER can move in a flat yard more reliably without unsafe behavior.

## Scope
- Improve flat-yard wander target selection.
- Improve short grass handling for harvesting target detection.
- Improve wander stuck tolerance with bounded retry behavior.
- Improve yard diagnostics and stop logging.
- Preserve strict stop behavior and idle recovery.

## Non-Goals
- No AI connection or AI autonomy.
- No free roaming outside yard radius.
- No mining/harvesting/combat/building/containers during wander.
- No infinite retries or autonomous loops.

## Required Safety Posture
- Owner-commanded only for wander controls.
- Keep `ALLOW_WANDER=false` default.
- Keep dangerous capabilities gated by env and default OFF.
- Wandering remains bounded by duration, steps, and yard radius.

## Required Env Flags
- `ALLOW_WANDER=false`
- `WANDER_OWNER_ONLY=true`
- `WANDER_CENTER_MODE=home`
- `WANDER_RADIUS=8`
- `WANDER_MAX_DURATION_MS=30000`
- `WANDER_STEP_RADIUS=4`
- `WANDER_PAUSE_MS=2000`
- `WANDER_MAX_STEPS=8`
- `WANDER_TARGET_ATTEMPTS=20`
- `WANDER_FLAT_ONLY=true`
- `WANDER_MAX_STUCK_RETRIES=1`
- `WANDER_STOP_ON_DANGER=true`
- `WANDER_STOP_ON_LOW_HEALTH=true`
- `WANDER_LOW_HEALTH_THRESHOLD=10`
- `WANDER_STOP_ON_LOW_FOOD=true`
- `WANDER_LOW_FOOD_THRESHOLD=8`
- `WANDER_ALLOW_MINING=false`
- `WANDER_ALLOW_HARVEST=false`
- `WANDER_ALLOW_COMBAT=false`
- `WANDER_ALLOW_BUILDING=false`
- `WANDER_ALLOW_CONTAINERS=false`
- `WANDER_REQUIRE_HOME=true`
- `WANDER_RESPECT_HOME_PROTECTION=true`

## Commands in Scope
- `Ember wander`
- `Ember wander home`
- `Ember wander stop`
- `Ember yard status`
- `Ember yard check`
- Existing `Ember stop` behavior must stop wander immediately.

## Acceptance Criteria
- With wander OFF: `Ember wander` reports disabled.
- With wander ON in flat yard: EMBER can complete at least 1-3 steps more reliably.
- If stuck, EMBER stops safely, reports reason, and returns to `idle`.
- No mining/harvesting/combat/building/container actions occur while wandering.
- `Ember yard check` includes flat mode and target-attempt diagnostics.
